abstract: >-
  cryoFM learns a generative prior from high-quality cryo-EM density maps using flow matching. A Bayesian framework combines this prior with experimental constraints to restore and refine maps affected by noise, uneven sampling, and reconstruction artifacts. The model supports posterior sampling and conditional post-processing, improving map interpretability across varied experimental settings.
